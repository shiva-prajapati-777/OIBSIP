📖 About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

He made significant contributions to India's space and defence programmes and was widely known as the "People's President". He continues to inspire students and young people through his emphasis on education, science, innovation and dreaming big.

🕰️ Timeline
1931 — Born in Rameswaram
1960 — Joined DRDO
1969 — Joined ISRO
1980 — SLV-III Mission
1997 — Awarded Bharat Ratna
2002 — Became the 11th President of India
2015 — Passed away while teaching students
