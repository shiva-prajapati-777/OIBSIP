01 / HIS LIFE

From a small town to the nation.

His journey reflected determination, curiosity, humility and an unwavering belief in India's potential.

01

Early Life & Education

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam grew up in a modest family.

His early life shaped his discipline, curiosity and commitment to learning.

02 / TIMELINE

Milestones of a remarkable life.

Click any milestone to explore the journey.

Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
He began his career as a scientist at the Defence Research and Development Organisation.
Kalam joined the Indian Space Research Organisation and contributed to India's space programme.
The successful SLV-III mission placed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.
He was awarded the Bharat Ratna for his contribution to science and public service.
Kalam became the 11th President of India and served from 2002 to 2007.
Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while doing what he loved — teaching students.
